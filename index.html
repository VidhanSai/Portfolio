How we’ll work together

A simple process with no surprises.

Brief

We talk about what the video is for, who’s watching and what you want them to feel.

Direction

I share references and a rough cut structure so we agree on the look early.

Edit and revise

You get drafts, I take your notes seriously, and we refine until it feels right.

Delivery

Clean exports in the formats you need, ready to post anywhere.

I care about every frame

I got into editing because I love the moment a rough cut turns into something that gives you goosebumps, and I’ve been chasing that feeling ever since.

Whether it’s a short reel or a full brand film, I think about pacing, emotion and music, and about what the viewer will feel ten seconds from now.
